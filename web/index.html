Only clean money reaches you.

The name comes from kiyoi, Japanese for clean. That's the whole idea: what reaches you is only what you said yes to.

What keeps it clean

  • Screening

    Addresses known for fraud are blocked. Payments from them can only be declined.

  • Reports

    If a payment looks wrong, report the sender. Each payment can be reported once, by the person who received it.

  • 5 of 7 reviewers

    Seven reviewers look at reported senders. When five agree, the sender is blocked for everyone.

  • Public record

    Every accept, decline and block is recorded on the blockchain and can never be edited.
